Tennis IQ Privacy Policy

Tennis IQ works without an account. The app does not send your scores or gameplay to us and does not include advertising, tracking or third party analytics.

Stored on your device

Your best rally score, number of completed sessions, best streak, personal tennis-knowledge rating, earned badges, 20 most recent rounds and first-attempt answer history are saved locally. Daily Rally also contributes to this local progress. Earned badges persist within your local progress. The knowledge rating describes performance on tennis questions; it is not a playing-ability rating or psychological IQ score. Apple’s device backup services may include this data according to your device settings. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes local data. Restoring a backup may restore it.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time in-app purchase. We do not receive your payment card details. The app uses Apple’s verified purchase information to unlock paid features and restore access. Apple handles purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Sharing you choose

If you choose to share a result card, the app opens Apple’s share sheet with an image containing your round result and knowledge rating. You choose the destination and recipients, and can cancel. The service you choose handles shared content according to its own privacy practices.

If you share a friend challenge, the link contains the selected question identifiers, question-bank version, link-format version and a compatibility hash. It does not contain your identity, score, rating or readable answers. A recipient with the same question bank and full-game access can use the link to play the same questions. Challenges and results are not sent to a Tennis IQ server; there are no server leaderboards or verified online results.

Support correspondence

If you email michael@srq.tennis, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond and resolve your request. Email is handled by our email provider. Do not send sensitive information. You can ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to legal retention requirements.

Your choices

No personal information is required to play. We do not sell gameplay data or use it for advertising. These practices apply to all players, including children. A parent or guardian can contact us about a child’s support correspondence.

This website

Visiting this page sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to the website hosting provider to deliver the page. This page does not embed analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy. For privacy questions, email michael@srq.tennis.
