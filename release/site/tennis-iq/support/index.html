Tennis IQ Support

Need help or found a question that needs correcting? Email michael@srq.tennis with the question text, your device model and what happened. Please do not include payment details or sensitive personal information.

What is free?

Daily Rally is free forever. Each round has ten mixed questions with an explanation after every answer. No purchase is required to play Daily Rally. Daily Rally also contributes to your progress stored on this device.

What does the unlock include?

One optional purchase unlocks Shot Clock, Practice, the full question library, My IQ, the 30-question placement round, result cards and friend challenges. There is no subscription. The app shows the price in your App Store currency before you buy.

Restore your purchase

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Open About, Support & Privacy in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Restore is also available on the unlock screen. A connection to the App Store may be needed.

Practice and Shot Clock

Practice lets you select a topic and difficulty with no timer. Shot Clock gives you twenty seconds per question. Its timer continues while you leave the app.

My IQ and placement

My IQ is a personal tennis-knowledge rating based on your answers in the app. It is not a rating of your tennis playing ability or a psychological IQ score. The placement round has 30 questions. My IQ also shows earned badges and your 20 most recent rounds. Badges remain earned in your local progress.

Scores and progress

Your rating, badges, recent rounds and first-attempt answer history are stored on your device. Daily Rally contributes to local progress even without the unlock; viewing My IQ and playing placement require the full-game purchase. Purchase access can be restored through Apple, but local progress does not automatically sync between devices. Deleting the app can remove that progress.

Share a result

Use the result-card sharing control to open Apple’s share sheet. Choose where to send or save the image, or cancel. The card includes your round result and knowledge rating. Sharing a card requires the full-game unlock.

Challenge a friend

Share a 10-question challenge link, then have your friend open it in Tennis IQ or paste the full link into the challenge screen. Both players need the full-game unlock and the same question-bank version. A challenge preserves the same questions in the same order. If a link is incomplete or the question bank differs, update both apps and share a new link.

Challenges are friendly, unverified rounds. There is no server leaderboard or online verification of results. Links use the tennisiq://challenge format and contain question identifiers and a compatibility hash, without identity information or readable answers.

Refunds

Apple handles App Store purchases and refund requests. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com for Apple’s refund process. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, paid access may be removed; Daily Rally remains free.

Independent tennis knowledge

Tennis IQ is an independent quiz app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the ITF, ATP, WTA or Grand Slam tournaments. Historical records use the cutoff stated in each question. Tactical advice depends on the situation.
