## Promotional text

## Description
Know the court. Win the argument.

Tennis IQ tests what you know about tennis, from scoring and court rules to Grand Slam history and the language of the game. Every answer comes with an explanation.

DAILY RALLY IS FREE FOREVER
Play a round of ten mixed questions. No purchase is needed, and there are no ads.

UNLOCK THE FULL GAME
An optional one-time in-app purchase adds:
• Shot Clock: twenty seconds per question, with bonus points for quick answers.
• Practice: choose a topic and difficulty, then learn at your own pace.
• Full Library: browse 1,584 questions, answers and explanations.
• My IQ: establish a knowledge baseline with placement, track a local rating and earn badges.
• Share result cards and send a friend the same ten-question challenge through a link.

The solo game requires no subscription. The purchase price is shown in your local App Store currency before you buy. Previous purchases can be restored using the same Apple Account.

PLAY WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Your scores, answer history, rating and badges stay on your device. The rating measures quiz knowledge, not psychological IQ or playing ability. Replaying familiar questions does not boost it. Challenge links work between matching versions of Tennis IQ and are friendly, unverified games, without an online leaderboard. Nothing is shared automatically. Gameplay works offline; purchases and restoring access may require an App Store connection.

Tennis IQ is an independent quiz app, not an official product of any tennis governing body or tournament. Historical records identify their era or cutoff where needed. Coaching tips depend on the situation.

## Review notes
No login or demo account is required. Daily Rally is free and does not require purchase. From the home screen, Shot Clock, Practice, Library, My IQ and Challenge open the purchase sheet until the nonconsumable Full Game Unlock is owned. The purchase sheet displays Apple's localized price. Restore Purchases is available there and under About, Support & Privacy. After purchase, those features open directly. My IQ offers a thirty-question untimed placement and earned badges. A completed round can generate a result card; sharing is user initiated. Challenge accepts a tennisiq://challenge# link containing ten question IDs and a content/version check; it needs no server. The app includes the question bank and gameplay works offline. No web checkout or external payment is used by the native app.
